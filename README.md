# Every Drop — Clean Water for Everyone

A child-friendly interactive story about safe, reliable and fair access to water. Conservation is one supporting chapter, rather than the sole cause or solution to the water crisis.

Serve `dist/` with any static HTTP server. No build step or accounts are required. Fonts and artwork are bundled locally. No visitor data is collected.

## Explore

- The main MIN–MAX slider continuously adjusts game flow from 0 to 12 L/min, including intermediate values. Both nature views and the advanced slider stay in sync.

- Toggle four parts of a clean-water journey: reliable supply, appropriate treatment and testing, safe storage, and lasting care. The route and progress update with the selected steps. The reset clears this learning activity.
- Reveal illustrated germs in a clear-looking bucket to learn that appearance alone does not establish drinking-water safety.
- Explore keyboard-accessible Families, Animals, and Trees tabs.
- Read linked facts with their dates, geographic scope, and definitions. The 1.4 million estimate covers unsafe water, sanitation and hygiene in 2019. Animal and tree figures describe historical regional droughts, not yearly global totals.
- Open the separate conservation game to adjust the flow slider, choose activities, earn badges, and compare useful and unnecessary water use.
- Save the conservation scene as a 3840 × 2160 JPEG, enlarged from the generated artwork.

The access route is a learning illustration, not a safety assessment or a claim of actual services delivered. The conservation garden changes at an accelerated rate. Its water totals and badges are game calculations, with progress reset on reload.

## Sources shown in the app

- WHO and UNICEF, 2025 report on safely managed drinking water, with 2024 estimates.
- WHO, burden of disease attributable to unsafe water, sanitation and hygiene, 2019 estimates.
- Acted, 2020–2023 Horn of Africa drought crisis and livestock losses.
- Texas A&M Forest Service, estimated tree deaths in the 2011 Texas drought.
- CDC, safe water storage.
